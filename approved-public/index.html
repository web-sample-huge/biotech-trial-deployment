01Origin

The Beginning of Transformation

Every transformation begins with seeing value where others see waste. For us, that journey began with chicken feathers and human hair — two overlooked materials rich in structural proteins, holding the potential for a remarkable second life.

Branched feather structure

A feather has a central shaft with fine branches called barbs. Structural proteins help give it its shape.

02Structure

Different by Nature. Valuable by Design.

Look closer, and nature reveals two extraordinary structures. A feather branches into delicate barbs, while human hair forms continuous keratin-rich fibres. Understanding these structures became the foundation of our approach to transforming them.

Branched feather structure

A feather has a central shaft with fine branches called barbs. Structural proteins help give it its shape.

03Transformation

Where Complexity Becomes Possibility

Through hydrolysis and our proprietary processing approach, complex proteins are broken down into smaller fragments. Materials once considered difficult to repurpose become the foundation of a new agricultural formulation.

Conceptual visualization of material change.

04Refinement

Turning Research into Formulation

Years of research begin to take shape in liquid form. Through formulation development and quality evaluation, we bring together material science and the practical requirements of agriculture.

05Agriculture

Created with Agriculture in Mind

Every crop has its own nutritional requirements, growing conditions and challenges. Our approach connects biotechnology with these differences, developing formulations with the needs of individual crops and growers in mind.

The root scene illustrates the growing environment. The particles are symbolic.

06Field research

Science That Reaches the Field

The journey that began with overlooked materials ultimately returns to nature. Field observations, crop evaluation and the experience of growers help guide the continuing development of our agricultural formulations.

Spraying method

Mix 1 litre of product with 10 litres of water and spray.

Drip irrigation / 1 acre

Mix 5 liters of fertilizer with 200 liters of water to use.

Seed treatment

Use 10 ml of liquid fertilizer per 1 kg of seeds. Soak the seeds in this solution for 30 minutes and dry them in shade for 15 to 30 minutes before planting.

Results may vary depending on soil type and environmental conditions.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep the container tightly closed when not in use.
